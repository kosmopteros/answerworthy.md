# The Answerworthy Standard

**Make your business easier to find, understand and choose.**

Give this free Standard to a capable agent. Start with the business material you already have. Investigate the public presence, prepare or perform worthwhile improvements, and keep the work in a portable `answerworthy.md` that another person or agent can continue.

SEO makes you findable. GEO makes you citable. **Answerworthy makes you choosable for the right buyer, for reasons the evidence supports.**

This is Answerworthy **1.0**. The canonical specification is maintained in [`answerworthy/answerworthy.md`](https://github.com/answerworthy/answerworthy.md). Its starter and worked example use the same version. Earlier drafts remain in Git history.

## Quick start

Paste this into a capable agent:

```text
Get started with https://answerworthy.md
```

If no business material is already available, the agent asks for one useful source: a website, deck, document, PDF, product page, profile, repository, or short description. You do not need to fill out a template first.

A useful first session leaves you with an improvement performed or usable material prepared, the specific facts or decisions still needed, and a clear next step. See the [worked business file](examples/acme/answerworthy.md) and its [self-contained fictional source pack](examples/acme/materials.md). They demonstrate the work and continuation, not a measured customer result.

The Standard is usable without the hosted Read, the paid Plan, proprietary prompts or a particular model. The **Answerworthy Plan** is a separate one-time organic-growth engagement: it uses the Standard alongside broader business and market research, commercial judgment and preparation. The Standard does not withhold useful Actions to protect that service.

## What Answerworthy means

A business is **Answerworthy within a declared scope** when its public decision corpus gives a capable answering system accessible, consistent, and verifiable grounds to decide whether the business is a defensible fit for a prospective customer's question — including clear grounds not to choose it when the fit is wrong.

The unit is contextual:

> **business × buyer-question family × market × language × constraints × time**

The **public decision corpus** is all public information from which a system can identify, understand, compare, and justify a decision about the business: owned pages and data, public profiles and documentation, marketplaces, reviews, publications, case studies, directories, and other relevant external sources.

The business's objective, current offer, existing audience and useful assets guide which buyer decisions are worth improving. Explain how the selected Outcomes contribute to that objective. Owner-supplied knowledge is usable business context; record its source. A private document does not become public evidence merely because an agent reads it.

The corpus is **sufficient within scope** when no material decision fact or established buyer-question family remains absent, vague, contradictory, unsupported, or stripped of its context. A fact or question is material when it could change fit, comparative choice, terms, risk, or the buyer's next action.

An exact query is a test or language sample. A **question family** is the coverage unit: several phrasings may express the same underlying buying decision. The Standard does not require one page per query.

## Compression and decision-grade expansion

Human-facing marketing may compress. A headline, name, or short proposition is an interface for people; it does not need to carry the whole buying case.

Every consequential meaning it compresses must resolve through the public decision corpus into decision-grade expansion: the precise offer, fit and non-fit, terms, constraints, alternatives, evidence, and limits needed for the buyer situation.

Preserve useful concise expression. Create, improve or connect the detail needed to understand it. The expansion must clarify, not materially revise, the impression created by the compression. Assessment commentary belongs in the working record unless it helps the public reader make the decision.

## The business-owned file

`answerworthy.md` is the business-owned, portable working file defined by this Standard. The starter and a mature guide are the same artifact at different stages.

It grows with business-specific sources, Scope, buyer Outcomes, observed State, selected Actions and their prepared material, and maintenance information. Keep large research sets, raw observations and transcripts elsewhere; link the material necessary for continuation.

The fixed section order is:

```markdown
---
answerworthy: "1.0"
standard: "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/answerworthy/answerworthy.md/main/README.md"
---

## Sources
## Scope
## Outcomes
## State
## Actions
## Maintenance
```

Read the living `standard:` reference before continuing. Also record the revision or dated release used for the work. On a later Standard revision, review compatibility explicitly; retain the original interpretation and permissions rather than silently restamping historical work.

The file can contain prepared artifacts beneath their Actions or reference accessible companion files. It must not depend on the original conversation, a vendor account or hidden instructions to explain the next operation. Do not copy the whole Standard or an implementation manual into every business file.

## Runtime contract

When an agent applies this Standard, it must:

1. **Read the current Standard.** Resolve the existing file's living reference and retain the version used for this session.
2. **Accept a zero-input start.** If no business source is available, ask one question in the user's language: “Send one useful source about the business — a URL, document, profile, repository, or short description.”
3. **Continue existing work.** Find and read an existing `answerworthy.md` and its relevant linked materials. Otherwise create the six-section file; return it as a named Markdown artifact when persistence is unavailable.
4. **Understand before selecting work.** Inspect the available business material and relevant public sources. Establish the objective and operating context from available information. Discover buyer situations, existing assets, question families, markets, languages, alternatives, criteria, constraints and material non-fit. Use further owner questions only for consequential information the agent cannot establish.
5. **Keep provenance visible.** Distinguish supplied knowledge, observations, research, interpretation and proposals. Neither repetition nor model agreement upgrades an inference into a fact.
6. **Select a bounded workstream.** Define one to four falsifiable buyer Outcomes and their contribution to the business objective. Include material non-fit where it changes a decision. Outcomes are not rankings, scores or content tasks. Make the available effort and scope boundary explicit.
7. **Inspect the relevant corpus against the eight conditions.** Record both assessment coverage and what the sources establish. Uninspected information is not evidence of its absence.
8. **Recommend worthwhile work.** Work backwards from the Outcomes. Consider preserving, improving, connecting and reusing existing material as well as creating something new. An opportunity can advance an Outcome without requiring a negative State. Explain the factual premises and professional judgment behind the selection.
9. **Prepare or perform the selected work.** Include the actual draft, populated structure, patch, research result or other usable work product, or an accessible reference. A deliverable's name is not its preparation. Identify only the genuinely missing inputs needed for the next operation.
10. **Apply permission to the actual operation.** Use permissions already granted. Obtain the required human approval before publishing new or materially changed consequential claims or sending/submitting work to third parties. A later approval does not prevent independent research and preparation now.
11. **Verify what changed.** Check completed implementation separately from subsequent buyer or answering-system responses. Preparing a draft does not establish changed public State or commercial success.
12. **Leave a usable continuation.** Reconcile the current file, preserve useful history, and identify the next operation, materials and unresolved decisions. Continue ready material work within the agreed workstream, effort, tools and permission; close the session when that boundary is reached.

Inspect before asking. When tools or access are insufficient, identify the affected research or operation, retain what is usable, and leave the precise continuation. An unavailable collector is not a defect in the business.

## Scope provenance

Use these values for material Scope premises:

- **`supplied`** — stated by the business, operator, or supplied material. It can establish business-side knowledge; it does not by itself establish public visibility or measured market demand.
- **`observed`** — directly present in retained buyer, customer, market, or public evidence.
- **`researched`** — supported by external research rather than direct observation in the inspected business context.
- **`inferred`** — an interpretation or working hypothesis derived from available information.

A recommendation can use reasoned judgment before its result has been measured. Record the factual premises, rationale and proposed work separately from observed results. Inference can guide useful preparation and experiments as well as further investigation.

Mark Sources public or private. Private knowledge can inform Scope and proposals; it satisfies a public-corpus condition only when the relevant information is publicly inspectable.

## The eight conditions

These form a dependency network, not a score, a report template or a universal implementation order.

### 1 · Reachable

**Can relevant systems and buyers access the decision-useful information?**

Required facts and evidence must be available through the surfaces, formats and access conditions relevant to Scope. Rendered/raw HTML, profiles, structured data, feeds and other retrieval paths are implementation choices, not universal requirements.

### 2 · Identified

**Can the business be resolved as one consistent entity?**

Names, aliases, people, products, locations and relationships must be unambiguous and materially consistent across the corpus. Record uncertainty rather than silently choosing between conflicting identities.

### 3 · Understood

**Is the offer and comparative fit explicit?**

The corpus must explain what is offered, to whom, where, on what terms, under which constraints, and where it is a strong or weak fit against relevant alternatives. Use buyer decision language. Concise marketing can remain concise when its consequential meaning resolves into precise public detail.

### 4 · Answering

**Does the corpus answer the material questions buyers use before knowing the business's name?**

Coverage follows question families in the declared market and language, including local terminology and materially different constraints. Answers should contain decision-useful distinctions and remain intelligible away from their original page. No single page must carry the entire explanation.

### 5 · Evidenced

**Can consequential reasons for choosing or rejecting the business be checked?**

Use the provenance, scope, freshness, method and limitations necessary for the particular assertion and its use. An offer term, credential, product specification and measured comparative result call for different support. Assess the meaning, not a count of links or preferred phrases. Preserve clear, persuasive expression while making decision-relevant support available.

### 6 · Corroborated

**Which material facts survive beyond the business's own assertion?**

Use independent sources for facts for which self-publication is insufficient. A third-party page that repeats the business's wording does not independently establish the underlying claim.

### 7 · Distributed

**Are useful answers and evidence present where this market encounters them?**

Consider relevant owned and external surfaces. Existing material may need a better connection or distribution route rather than rewriting. Select destinations for the audience and contribution, not to fill a channel quota.

### 8 · Observed

**What do particular answering systems say under documented conditions?**

Retain the exact question, provider/model where available, mode, date, locale, relevant context, representation, position, buyer fit, stated reasons and their support in the corpus. Record whether the business was named in the input. Prompted brand research and a fresh unprompted buyer question establish different observations.

One run is not a stable position. Stated reasons do not expose hidden retrieval, ranking, training influence or internal reasoning. Within the inspected scope, assess whether the system chooses or rejects the business for an appropriate buyer and supportable reasons. Record access gaps separately from the observed answer.

## File semantics

### Sources

List material inputs and evidence needed to continue. Mark access, ownership or attribution, and inspection date where relevant. Keep quotations faithful. A reference should resolve to the actual source or an accessible retained excerpt.

### Scope

Record the business objective, operating context, selected workstream and effort boundary alongside buyer situations, question families, market, language, alternatives, criteria, constraints, non-fit and assessment coverage. Preserve provenance. An unspecified objective remains an explicit provisional premise rather than a claimed instruction from the owner.

### Outcomes

Use stable IDs such as `O1`. Describe the buyer decision and how it supports the business objective:

> **O1 — QF1:** A small German team comparing analytics tools can determine whether Acme supports its required roles and deployment arrangements, and take the appropriate next step. This supports the objective of increasing suitable trial enquiries.

Include a non-fit Outcome where an incorrect recommendation would materially matter. A business may have wider growth objectives than the buyer Outcomes addressed by this workstream.

### State

Use stable IDs, a condition, an explicit assessment state, supporting sources and any material unresolved information.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `verified` | Current inspectable evidence supports the stated conclusion within the recorded scope. |
| `unsupported` | An assertion was identified but its necessary support has not been established in the inspected material. This does not mean it is false. |
| `contradicted` | Inspected evidence materially conflicts with the stated assertion; identify both and the conflict. |
| `missing` | Adequate inspection of the relevant scope establishes that required information or a condition is absent. |
| `unreached` | The relevant information or observation could not be accessed or performed. |
| `stale` | Previously useful evidence falls outside the recorded freshness requirement or has been superseded. |
| `incomplete` | Part is established and specific material requirements remain unresolved. |

`coverage: full`, `bounded` or `uncovered` describes the scope of inspection, not another assessment state. An uncovered condition remains unassessed; a failed fetch does not establish `missing` or `contradicted`. Dates and context belong to the underlying evidence.

```markdown
### S1 — Team-role explanation
condition: understood
coverage: bounded
state: verified
evidence:
  - product guide explains viewer and administrator responsibilities; SRC-2
```

A proposal belongs under Actions. Revising an interpretation is different from changing public reality; retain the superseded interpretation when useful.

### Actions

An Action is a worthwhile operation advancing an Outcome, resolving a material blocker or verifying a result. It can involve business clarification, product/service work, research, evidence production, publication, corroboration, distribution or technical access. It does not require inventing a deficit.

Use stable IDs and the actual next operation. Separate the intended contribution, material used or produced, necessary prerequisites and verification. `needs:` records actors; it does not automatically determine a waiting state.

```markdown
### A1 — Prepare a clear team-role explanation
advances: [O1]
serves: [understood, answering]
state: done
needs: [agent]
operation: prepare page passage
prerequisites: []
why: connect existing documented roles to the buyer's deployment decision
prepared_material: ./drafts/team-roles.md
done_when:
  - the populated draft uses the documented roles and is ready for factual approval
verify:
  - compare the draft with SRC-2 and open the referenced artifact
next_operation: obtain approval to publish the passage
```

This snippet illustrates the shape; the worked example supplies the material itself. Inline prepared content is equally valid. Use no compulsory length or artifact count. An independently executable Action may have no missing inputs.

| Action state | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ready` | The next operation can proceed with the available inputs, tools and permission. |
| `blocked` | A named necessary prerequisite prevents that operation. |
| `waiting-human` | A specified human fact, decision or permission is needed for that operation. |
| `waiting-third-party` | A specified external response or operation is needed next. |
| `done` | The named operation met its completion condition; this does not claim a later operation or business effect occurred. |

Preserve independent work when a particular operation waits. An approved upstream artifact is consumed directly by later work; the owner should not need to copy it between tasks. Prepared material must travel with the handoff, inline or at an accessible reference.

### Maintenance

Record the Standard revision used and review the living source on continuation. Recheck after material changes to the offer, corpus, market or evidence. Verify facts needed before relying on prior work. Preserve useful history without leaving superseded instructions active. Record completed work, remaining decisions and the next useful step within the session boundary.

## Rules

1. **Purpose guides the work.** Tie selected buyer Outcomes and Actions to the business's objective and circumstances.
2. **Premises retain provenance.** Supplied knowledge, observations, research, judgment and proposals have distinct roles. Unknown is valid.
3. **Compression resolves into explanation.** Preserve useful expression and connect the decision-relevant detail without changing the impression covertly.
4. **Support is proportionate.** Assess consequential assertions using evidence appropriate to their meaning and use. Writing a claim does not make it true.
5. **No fabrication or strengthening.** Do not invent sources, dates, quotations, reviews, measurements, demand or certainty.
6. **Independence is real.** No fabricated reviews, astroturfing, self-review posing as independence, or access-control bypass. Repetition alone is not corroboration.
7. **Measurements serve the objective.** Counts, positions and checks are evidence, not a reason to create work by themselves.
8. **Observation is not hidden causality.** Record what the system said and the conditions; distinguish implementation completion from later observed response.
9. **Questions earn interruption.** Ask only for a material unresolved input the agent cannot establish and that affects the work.
10. **Authority follows the operation.** Respect granted permissions and obtain missing approval at consequential publication, submission or contact. Keep preparation and execution distinct.
11. **Leave useful work.** Perform or prepare the selected operation and leave a coherent continuation, rather than only a list of assignments.
