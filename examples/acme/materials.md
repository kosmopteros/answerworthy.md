# Acme example source pack

This is a fictional teaching fixture, not research about a real company, observed market demand or evidence of customer results. The entries marked public represent the supplied public corpus for the exercise; example URLs must not be fetched as real sources. All scenario dates are 12 September 2026.

## SRC-1 — Owner brief (private, supplied)

Acme Analytics GmbH sells analytics software to small German teams. The business objective for this workstream is more suitable trial enquiries. Its homepage and product guide already explain the core product. The owner wants to improve the link between those explanations and the team's decision to try it. This is one preparation session, not a full-market research project.

The agent may read this pack and prepare materials. Publication and third-party contact are not yet authorised. The owner will review factual wording. Whether the current plan includes an implementation call is not resolved here. The agent must ask about that offer term, not invent it.

## SRC-2 — Product guide (public, supplied fixture)

Acme records website events and presents shared dashboards. Administrators configure data collection and team access. Viewers read dashboards without changing collection settings. The product guide explains these roles with a configuration walkthrough. Data for this offer is hosted in Germany. No compliance certification is asserted by this fixture. The current product does not include organisation-wide approval workflows.

Existing headline: “Analytics your whole team can understand.” The guide is useful existing material, not a blank page needing replacement.

## SRC-3 — Trial page (public, supplied fixture)

Prospective customers can request a trial using /trial. The form asks for a business email and a description of the use case. It does not require payment. The page links to the product guide but does not summarise the roles or make the organisation-wide approval-workflow boundary easy to find.

## SRC-4 — Existing partner's editorial invitation (public, supplied fixture)

The partner's newsletter welcomes practical vendor-authored explanations for small-team operators. Contributions are reviewed by its editor, labelled with authorship and may link to a relevant product guide. Acceptance is not automatic. No recipient address or available issue date is supplied.
